## 6. Case Classes

### 6.1 Positive case

A positive case demonstrates that the relevant PATH claim survives source, type, relation, Loss, Band, and governance pressure within its declared scope.

```text
positive case
≠ maximum-strength claim
```

A positive case may result in `admissible_with_bounded_claim` or `admissible_but_provisional` rather than `admissible`.

### 6.2 Countercase

A countercase begins from a plausible but inadmissible or overextended PATH construction and demonstrates exactly where the claim fails, reduces, stops, or becomes non-capturable.

```text
countercase
≠ obviously bad example
```

Its value lies in discriminating the boundary, not merely showing a caricature.

### 6.3 Confusion case

A confusion case holds two or more nearby interpretations open long enough to determine which operation, object class, frame, resolution, or output mapping is actually warranted.

```text
confusion case
≠ permission to collapse categories
```

A confusion case may resolve to one route, multiple bounded routes, Claim Reduction, `mandatory_stop`, or `non_capture`.

## 8. Positive Case Set

### 8.1 Simple Linear Path

Burden:

- clear but bounded order;
- few transitions;
- low branching;
- verifiable events;
- limited Path claim;
- no automatic Trajectory or dependence claim.

### 8.2 Branching Path

Burden:

- historically available continuations;
- source-supported Branch Point;
- realized and rejected or blocked continuations;
- changed later reachability;
- alternatives retained in the record.

### 8.3 Trajectory with a Central Non-Event

Burden:

- warranted expected occurrence and window;
- supported non-realization;
- positive sub-events retained;
- sedimented `Λ` load;
- later attractor/asymmetry interaction without `PROJECT_AS` target-function assignment.

### 8.4 Similar End States, Different Histories

Burden:

- comparable present cuts;
- different costs, bindings, residues, or exit structures;
- strong Path-Dependence claim only where current-state sufficiency fails materially;
- no whole-object inflation from one dependent dimension.

### 8.5 Repeated Pattern with Weak Path Dependence

Burden:

- visible repetition;
- limited historical additional load;
- current state predominantly reconstructible from current conditions;
- reduction from strong dependence to weak order dependence or Trajectory-only finding.

## 9. Countercase Set

### 9.1 Chronology Presented as Path

Many events and correct dates are present, but there is no warrantable transition structure, selection relation, or additional praxeological purchase.

### 9.2 Macro-Label without Traceable Path

A strong aggregate label hides intermediate configurations, reversals, `Λ`, `Ω`, alternatives, and source sensitivity and exceeds the Traceability Ceiling.

### 9.3 Teleological Composition

The endpoint selects its own antecedents, alternatives disappear, and direction is converted into necessity, maturation, progress, decline, or convergence.

### 9.4 Composition through Omitted Asymmetry

Events are correctly ordered, but costs, access, exposure, reversibility, repair, evidence, or binding loads are homogenized.

### 9.5 False Central Non-Event

The expectation structure, window, or non-realization support is absent, yet ordinary absence or missing sources are promoted to central `Λ`.

---

## 10. Confusion Case Set

### 10.1 Path or Projection?

PATH reconstructs how an object developed. A claim that the resulting trajectory functions as a Frame, Macro-Event, Attractor, or other contextual function belongs to a later `PROJECT_AS` record.

### 10.2 Path or Resolution Drift?

Additional timepoints are added, but no compressed object is opened and no new transition structure or praxis finding appears. The result may be `resolution_neutral`; it is not automatically PATH or SUB.

### 10.3 Trajectory or Attractor?

A single historical development is distinguished from a recurring or contextually functioning course-form. Any Attractor function remains a later bounded projection.

## 15. Stop and Non-Capture

`mandatory_stop` applies where a failed or overextended PATH claim is retained for:

- contextual target-function assignment without `PROJECT_AS`;
- intervention, ranking, sanction, diagnosis, or legitimacy;
- irreversible classification;
- authority inheritance;
- source-indifferent macro-labeling presented as established history.

`non_capture` remains available where multiple source-supported Paths, periodizations, or branch reconstructions cannot be responsibly collapsed into one object.

```text
failed_transformation
≠ mandatory_stop
≠ non_capture
```

Optional Stop remains possible where the case has answered its declared question and further reconstruction adds no justified purchase.
