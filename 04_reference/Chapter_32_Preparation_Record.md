## 6. Scientific Hardening Controls

### 6.1 Anti-punctualization

Target-level event-like use must not erase source duration. The Macro-Event may occupy one analytical position in a wider Path while remaining internally extended.

```text
one target element
≠ one instant
≠ one act
≠ one responsible agent
```

### 6.2 Boundary independence and anti-retrospective periodization

Start and end must be supported by source-side transitions, phase relations, or bounded target function—not selected only because a later label, outcome, or canonical period name becomes convenient. Source-object warrant, boundary warrant, and target-function warrant remain separate.

### 6.3 Temporal evidence separation

The Record must distinguish:

- evidence for the source Trajectory and its internal phases;
- evidence for the wider target Path and transition function;
- later retrospective evidence used for periodization or naming.

Later period labels may guide testing. They may not silently substitute for source or target support.

### 6.4 Transition function without causal turning-point proof

A Macro-Event claim may state that the projected source marks or structures a transition in the wider Path. It does not thereby prove that the source alone caused the transition, that no other source was relevant, or that the later state was necessary.

### 6.5 Multi-resolution traceability

Compression is admissible only while internal duration, constitutive phases, relevant reversals, heterogeneous consequences, roles, costs, asymmetries, Non-Events, and inherited Loss remain inspectable at the source level.

### 6.6 Operation-chain integrity

```text
COMPOSE(source configurations and transitions)
→ Trajectory M

PROJECT_AS(Trajectory M, wider Path B)
→ Macro-Event function candidate
```

The two operations require separate occurrences, Records, Loss profiles, and results. A failed projection does not undo the source `COMPOSE` result automatically.

## 7. Section Architecture

### 32.1 Basic Claim

Establish the bounded family claim: a PATH-established Trajectory may perform a Macro-Event function when it serves as a source-traceable transition-bearing unit in a wider declared Path.

### 32.2 Macro-Event as Target Function

Specify the target function while preserving Trajectory as origin type, internal temporal extension, and non-primitive status.

### 32.3 Target Frame

Require a wider Path or temporal Frame with explicit granularity, relative level, time scope, roles, relevant before/after relation, target praxis dimensions, and Claim Ceiling.

### 32.4 Boundary Selection

Require source-supported start, end, constitutive phases, internal turning points, and relation to preceding/following developments. Reject label-only and outcome-driven periodization.

### 32.5 Internal Duration

Preserve the source Trajectory's duration, sequence, phase relations, and temporal evidence boundaries despite target-level compression.

### 32.6 Internal Heterogeneity

Retain relevant reversals, parallel subpaths, local differences, role distributions, costs, asymmetries, Non-Events, and heterogeneous consequences.

### 32.7 Event Function

Name the bounded transition work: changed possibilities, wider-path segmentation, transition-bearing contrast, or path-structuring effect. Separate function warrant from causal turning-point proof.

### 32.8 Punctualization Error

Represent failure or Claim Reduction where duration, phases, responsibilities, costs, or heterogeneous processes are falsely compressed into one instant or act.

### 32.9 Macro-Event versus COMPOSE

Keep source formation and target projection as separate operations and Records. A source Trajectory may be valid even where the Macro-Event projection fails.

### 32.10 Counterfactual Macro-Event Test

Test phase removal, boundary variation, internal reversal, target-frame variation, and alternative-source pressure without causal proof or automated periodization.

### 32.11 Failed Macro-Event Projection

Represent failure where target Frame, source-supported boundaries, internal trace, transition gain, sensitivity, operation separation, or responsible discrimination cannot be sustained.

## 10. Loss, Stop, Non-Capture, and Re-entry

Canonical Loss remains exactly:

```yaml
loss:
  preserved:
  compressed:
  excluded:
  uncertain:
  irrecoverable:
```

Internal duration, phase relations, and heterogeneity may be foregrounded or backgrounded, but visibility declarations do not create new Loss fields. Punctualization that makes source duration or responsibility structure unrecoverable may require Claim Reduction or Failure.

Mandatory Stop applies where continuation would require origin-type replacement, retrospective boundary invention, complete erasure of internal duration, fusion of `COMPOSE` and `PROJECT_AS`, untraceable transition effect, causal turning-point certainty, person-level responsibility assignment from macro-compression, legitimacy judgment, recommendation, sanction, or authority inheritance.

Non-Capture remains available where rival periodizations cannot be responsibly discriminated, internal reversals defeat a unitary event function, transition gain cannot be separated from target-frame choice, or the source cannot be compressed without destroying constitutive heterogeneity.

A material change in source Trajectory, source boundaries, target Path, target Frame, target granularity, role-set, time window, event function, periodization, evidence, or alternative source object requires a new claim and, where the source object changes, potentially a new prior PATH/`COMPOSE` occurrence.
