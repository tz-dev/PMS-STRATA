## Required boundaries

- Every Appendix returns to its canonical prose owner.
- Templates and schemas may constrain declared structure but cannot decide empirical truth, causality, normativity, diagnosis, or authority.
- Appendix A–N may not introduce a fourth operation, a new Output Class, a new PMS primitive, or authority inheritance.
- Missing case pressure remains an explicit bounded duty rather than a completed claim.
