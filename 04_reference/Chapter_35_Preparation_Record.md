## 6. Scientific Pressure and Counterfactual Architecture

The family method must remain vulnerable to at least the following pressures:

1. **Dependency-preservation pressure:** the claimed prominence must not delete the source conditions or canonical dependencies of Ω or Θ.
2. **Role variation:** weighting may differ across `role_r1` and `role_r2`; one aggregate profile may be overinclusive.
3. **Temporal variation:** prominence may be phase-bound, unstable, reversed, or absent outside the selected window.
4. **Modulator removal:** removing `m1`, `m2`, or `m3` may alter access, threshold, timing, persistence, or target effect.
5. **Modulator substitution:** a different condition may carry the same effect, showing redundancy or misidentified load.
6. **Dominance/exclusivity pressure:** a prominent operator pair may coexist with constitutive load from other operators.
7. **Profile stability:** a rhetorically compact label may fail to describe a stable relation topology.
8. **Functional-inertness pressure:** a stable descriptive profile may make no additional later-praxis difference.
9. **Rival-profile pressure:** several non-equivalent profile descriptions may fit the same source.
10. **Single-occurrence sufficiency:** one operator occurrence or one narrower relation may carry all target work.
11. **Present-condition pressure:** current target conditions may explain `K` more economically than source profile `P`.
12. **Label-invariance pressure:** renaming or stylistically changing the profile label must not alter the substantive test.
13. **Sampling and evidence pressure:** missing, weak, or selectively visible operator traces may manufacture apparent weighting.
14. **Person-typing pressure:** configuration language must not migrate to persons, groups, institutions, cultures, or identities.
15. **Add-on pressure:** AXIOM, STACK, EM, DISCIPLINE, EDEN, SEX, or another add-on may stress-test but cannot define the weighting or profile.

Counterfactual Sensitivity remains qualitative and source-bound. No universal profile score, operator-weight vector, probability estimate, causal percentage, personality classifier, or automated label generator is permitted.
