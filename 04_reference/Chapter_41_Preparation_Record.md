## 7. Risk Taxonomy

Chapter 41 prepares five risk families without replacing the later detailed controls.

### 7.1 Infinite-decomposition risk

Potential symptoms:

- detail increases while PraxisPurchase declines;
- Source Ceiling is exceeded;
- source-object identity dissolves;
- counterexamples are buried in microcomplexity;
- finer resolution is assigned truth privilege.

### 7.2 Unlimited-composition risk

Potential symptoms:

- heterogeneous paths are homogenized;
- local contradiction and Loss disappear;
- source trace becomes non-reconstructible;
- a macro-label is mistaken for a function;
- larger scope is treated as greater authority.

### 7.3 Arbitrary-projection risk

Potential symptoms:

- any source can be assigned any function;
- Target Context moves after objections;
- analogy is promoted to function;
- origin type is replaced;
- target fit substitutes for Source Trace.

### 7.4 Vertical-authority risk

Potential symptoms:

- macro is treated as more true;
- micro is treated as more fundamental;
- more operations are treated as stronger evidence;
- model completeness is treated as application authority.

### 7.5 Self-immunization risk

Potential symptoms:

- failed claims relocate across Frame, level, granularity, composite, or function;
- prior dispositions disappear;
- every counterexample triggers a new variant;
- no stable failure condition survives;
- analysis becomes incapable of Stop or Non-Capture.
