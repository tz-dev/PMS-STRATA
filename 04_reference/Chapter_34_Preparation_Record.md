## 6. Scientific Pressure and Counterfactual Architecture

The family method must remain vulnerable to at least the following pressures:

1. **Mere aggregation:** many components coexist without coordination, reinforcement, dependency, sequence, or shared target effect.
2. **Frame-selection artifact:** a broad analytical Frame creates apparent unity that disappears under a justified rival Frame.
3. **Smaller-subset sufficiency:** a narrower subset carries all target work, making the larger composite overinclusive.
4. **Component substitution:** replacement or removal leaves the function intact, showing redundancy or misidentified load.
5. **Relation disruption:** components remain present while coordination or reinforcement is removed.
6. **Internal contradiction:** local structures weaken, redirect, cancel, or split the target function.
7. **Target variation:** the same source composite performs a function in `H` but not in `I`.
8. **Present-condition pressure:** current target conditions explain the function more economically than the source composite.
9. **Source heterogeneity and missingness:** uncertain, excluded, or inaccessible components may alter the formation claim.
10. **Alternative source object:** a narrower, wider, or differently bounded composite may carry the target work more responsibly.

Counterfactual Sensitivity remains qualitative, source-bound, and non-causal. No score, percentage, automated emergent-function classifier, or universal component threshold is permitted.
