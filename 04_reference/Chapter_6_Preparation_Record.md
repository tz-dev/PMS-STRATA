## 3. Contract Lock

### 3.1 Governing question

> When does additional resolution, composition, or functional projection produce a warranted praxeological distinction, and when does it become detail without purchase or abstraction without traceable load?

### 3.2 Required claims

Chapter 6 must establish:

1. the Admissibility Band is relational and claim-specific rather than a universal metric;
2. the lower boundary is the Praxeological Relevance Floor;
3. below the floor, a distinction may be correct yet add no warranted praxis finding;
4. praxis purchase concerns changed warranted reconstruction, not immediate actionability;
5. the upper boundary is the Praxeological Traceability Ceiling;
6. above the ceiling, a transformation result no longer carries reconstructible load from its declared sources;
7. citation or source listing alone does not establish TraceableLoad;
8. Counterfactual Sensitivity tests declared source dependence without proving causality;
9. Type Integrity and Contextual Boundedness remain required conditions inherited from Chapter 5;
10. all load-bearing conditions are non-compensatory;
11. the same object may support different admissibility results under different operations, contexts, claims, and source bases;
12. output classes remain distinct from local rule findings and require substantive routing;
13. no universal smallest relevant element, finest legitimate resolution, largest composite, or fixed projection threshold exists;
14. formal-model declarations may expose missing or incompatible fields but may not decide empirical relevance or semantic adequacy.

### 3.3 Required distinctions

| Distinction | Required Chapter 6 burden |
| --- | --- |
| detail vs. finding | identify the warranted statement that changes |
| relevance floor vs. source ceiling | lower boundary concerns purchase; source ceiling concerns support reach |
| traceability ceiling vs. citation | require structural mapping and source dependence |

### 3.4 Prohibited claims

- admissibility is a universal numeric score;
- strong performance on one condition cancels failure on another;
- more detail automatically passes the Relevance Floor;
- more citations automatically pass the Traceability Ceiling;
- Counterfactual Sensitivity proves causality or necessity;
- a formally valid record is substantively admissible;
- the band fixes universal thresholds for path, trajectory, attractor, macro-event, or projection;

## 5. Core Band Architecture

### 5.1 Admissibility assessment object

Admissibility is assessed for a delimited transformation claim, not for an object or concept globally.

```text
operation T
applied to source object X
within transformation context C
for claim Q
under source basis S
```

```text
Admissibility(T, X, C, Q, S)
```

The same source object may support one admissible transformation, another only after claim reduction, a resolution-neutral refinement, a failed transformation, or Non-Capture.

### 5.2 Praxeological Relevance Floor

The Relevance Floor is the lower boundary below which an added distinction, refinement, composition, or function claim creates no warranted change in the reconstruction of praxis-relevant structure.

Canonical formulation to preserve:

> **Below the Praxeological Relevance Floor, additional resolution is not an additional praxis finding. It is distinction without praxeological purchase.**

The floor does not ask whether the result is actionable, controllable, morally desirable, or immediately useful. It asks which warranted reconstruction changes.

### 5.3 Praxis-relevant dimensions

A distinction may possess purchase when it changes a warranted reconstruction concerning at least one of:

- available, constrained, or closed action corridors;
- distribution of costs;
- exposure or risk;
- roles and asymmetries;
- bindings and commitment structures;
- expectations and Non-Events;
- temporal order, duration, delay, or bounded irreversibility;
- path construction or path-dependence claims;
- target-function validity or recontextualization;
- integration possibilities;
- alternatives, claim scope, failure, or Stop conditions.

The list is open but controlled. A proposed new dimension must show praxeological purchase and may not become a new PMS primitive merely by being listed.

### 5.4 Changed-reconstruction test

The central lower-bound question is:

> **Which warranted statement must be formulated differently because of this distinction or transformation?**

Possible changed findings include typing, relation, cost, asymmetry, path construction, target function, scope, countercase, failure, or Stop. If no warranted statement changes, the result may be `resolution_neutral`, below-floor, underdetermined, or an optional/mandatory stop depending on support and continuation pressure.

```text
additional information
+ no changed warranted reconstruction
= resolution without praxeological purchase
```

### 5.5 Praxeological Traceability Ceiling

The Traceability Ceiling is the upper boundary above which a composition, decomposition, or projection no longer carries reconstructible structural load from its declared source objects.

Canonical formulation to preserve:

> **Above the Praxeological Traceability Ceiling, abstraction no longer carries reconstructible praxis load. It becomes a label without traceable source structure.**

The upper boundary may be crossed by macro-abstraction, fragmentation, or functional projection. “Higher” and “finer” are not intrinsically safe directions.

### 5.6 TraceableLoad

A transformation result possesses TraceableLoad when:

- constitutive source objects remain identifiable;
- load-bearing source features are declared;
- relevant relations and temporality remain reconstructible;
- constitutive costs, asymmetries, bindings, expectations, and Non-Events do not disappear;
- selection, compression, exclusion, uncertainty, and irrecoverable loss are disclosed;
- material changes in declared source load alter the result;
- the claim remains below its Source and Claim Ceilings.

```text
source citation
≠ structural mapping
≠ functional dependency
```

TraceableLoad does not require exhaustive reproduction. It requires enough preserved structure to reconstruct why the result depends on these sources rather than merely mentioning them.

### 5.7 Counterfactual Sensitivity

The core load question is:

> **Would a relevant change in a declared load-bearing source structure alter the composed object, decomposition finding, or projected function?**

Controlled findings may include:

```text
sensitive
partially sensitive
insensitive
underdetermined
not testable with available sources
```

Counterfactual Sensitivity is a dependency stress test. It does not prove actual causality, necessity, or what would empirically have happened in an unrealized world.

### 5.8 Type Integrity, Reference Continuity, and Contextual Boundedness

Chapter 6 consumes Chapter 5 rather than redefining it.

A transformation cannot enter the band when:

- the referent has silently changed;
- origin type is replaced by target function;
- a new primitive is invented by label substitution;
- relevant historical load is erased;
- local validity is transferred to another context without a new claim;
- authority is inherited from structural fit.

Strong purchase cannot compensate for failed Type Integrity. Strong source trace cannot compensate for unbounded context transfer.

### 5.9 Unified non-compensatory test

The compact foundational formula remains:

```text
Admissible(T, X, C)
iff
PraxisPurchase
and TraceableLoad
and TypeIntegrity
and ContextualBoundedness
```

The prose must make clear that Reference Continuity, Functional and Temporal Continuity, Counterfactual Sensitivity, loss, source/claim ceilings, Stop, and Non-Capture remain applicable supporting tests. The compact formula is not an exhaustive field list and not a score.

```text
high PraxisPurchase
+ failed TraceableLoad
= not admissible
```

```text
high TraceableLoad
+ no PraxisPurchase
= not an additional praxis finding
```

### 5.10 Non-symmetric boundary failures

The two boundaries fail differently:

| Boundary | Failure form | Typical retained remainder |
| --- | --- | --- |
| below Relevance Floor | distinction without purchase | coarser claim, `resolution_neutral`, optional/mandatory stop |
| above Traceability Ceiling | abstraction or fragmentation without load | weaker bounded label, `analogy_only`, claim reduction, failure, Non-Capture |

The band must not be represented as one symmetric distance from a central score.

### 5.11 Output-routing boundary

Rule findings and Output Classes remain separate axes.

Examples:

- `below_floor` is a local relevance finding, not automatically `resolution_neutral`;
- `insensitive` is a Counterfactual Sensitivity finding, not automatically `failed_transformation`;
- `stop_required: true` is a trigger, not automatically the canonical class until the delimited claim is routed;
- provisional support status does not automatically yield `admissible_but_provisional`;
- uncertainty does not automatically yield `non_capture`.

A canonical Output Class is selected only after claim segmentation, applicable-rule review, boundary adjudication, and substantive rationale.

### 5.12 No Universal STRATA Scale

There is no universal:

- smallest praxis-relevant element;
- finest admissible granularity;
- largest legitimate composite;
- maximum number of levels;
- fixed repetition threshold for attractor-function;
- fixed duration threshold for trajectory;
- fixed similarity threshold for projection.

Admissibility depends on frame, source object, source basis, granularity, relative level, temporal scope, transformation context, claim scope, and tested claim.

```text
relational boundary
≠ universal scale value
```

The Planck analogy remains heuristic only.

## 6. Non-Compensation and Claim Effects

### 6.1 Non-compensation matrix

| Strong condition | Failed condition | Required consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| high detail | PraxisPurchase | no resolution-gain claim |
| strong rhetoric or utility | TraceableLoad | reduce, fail, stop, or retain analogy only |
| rich source list | constitutive mapping | no traceability passage |
| clear referent | Type Integrity | reject hidden retyping |
| local functional fit | Contextual Boundedness | no cross-context transfer |
| valid earlier chain link | later-link admissibility | preserve earlier result; test later link separately |

Formal elegance, record completeness, citation volume, or model conformance may expose declarations. None can compensate for a failed substantive condition.

### 6.2 Candidate output effects

Chapter 6 must preserve all canonical classes without inventing aliases:

| Pattern | Candidate route, subject to full adjudication |
| --- | --- |
| all applicable burdens pass as declared | `admissible` |
| material scope restriction is decisive and retested | `admissible_with_bounded_claim` |
| coherent claim remains with material support uncertainty | `admissible_but_provisional` |
| genuine supported refinement changes no warranted reconstruction | `resolution_neutral` |
| bounded resemblance remains but projection fails | `analogy_only` |
| separable parts receive different outcomes | `partially_admissible` |
| current claim must be weakened and retested | `claim_reduction_required` |
| continuation crosses a mandatory boundary | `mandatory_stop` |
| declared operation itself fails | `failed_transformation` |
| available structure exceeds responsible capture | `non_capture` |

No row is an automatic keyword router.

### 6.3 Stop, failure, and Non-Capture

```text
mandatory_stop
→ continuation under present conditions is prohibited
```

```text
failed_transformation
→ the declared operation claim did not succeed
```

```text
non_capture
→ relevant structure remains beyond responsible selection or integration
```

Each may preserve earlier valid findings or weaker claims. None erases prior results.

### 7.1 Shared source family

### 7.2 Below-band example

A finer `DECOMPOSE` variant distinguishes several wording changes inside a meeting exchange. The changes are source-supported but alter no role, asymmetry, expectation, path, target function, claim scope, loss finding, or Stop condition.

Expected local finding:

```text

```

Candidate route:

```text
resolution_neutral
```

Continuation becomes unnecessary; insistence on indefinite further splitting may trigger Stop.

### 7.3 Within-band example

A bounded `DECOMPOSE` distinguishes the adoption event `E1`, the promised reconsideration expectation, the Non-Event `N1`, and the later transition `T2`. This changes the warranted reconstruction of reopening burden while preserving the same `P_review` referent, source trace, origin typing, temporal order, and claim scope.

Expected candidate:

```text
admissible_with_bounded_claim
```

The exact class remains subject to the fully drafted local test.

### 7.4 Above-band example

A `PROJECT_AS` claim labels `P_review` a universal “institutional attractor” across projects, teams, and periods. The label remains unchanged when `N1`, the promise, the temporal order, and the target context are materially altered.

```text
material source change
+ arbitrarily unchanged target label
→ abstraction without traceable load
```

Possible routes include `claim_reduction_required`, `failed_transformation`, `analogy_only`, or `mandatory_stop`, depending on the retained claim and insistence.

### 7.5 Non-actionable but relevant structure

A bounded irreversibility or closed alternative may pass the Relevance Floor even where no action can reopen the corridor. Praxis relevance concerns the warranted reconstruction of possibilities, costs, exposure, or commitments—not a requirement that the analyst can change the situation.

### 7.6 Source-limited pressure

A transformation may show clear purchase but only provisional traceability because one source interval is missing. The result may remain `admissible_but_provisional` only if one coherent delimited claim survives and the missing material is named. Missing data alone does not automatically produce Non-Capture.

### 7.7 Non-compensation pressure

An elegant macro-composite may have substantial interpretive utility while failing constitutive source reconstruction. Its usefulness cannot compensate for missing TraceableLoad.

### 7.8 Non-Capture pressure

Where two rival source-to-result mappings remain equally plausible and available sources cannot responsibly discriminate, the record preserves the rival reconstructions, limiting condition, and re-entry condition under `non_capture`. It does not protect a preferred synthesis.
