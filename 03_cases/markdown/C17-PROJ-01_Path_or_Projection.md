## 1. Confusion Claim

A five-stage coordination history is reconstructed as a Trajectory. Because a later governance meeting interprets current allocation through that history, the Trajectory is then described as already being a Frame.

The claim contains two analytically different questions:

1. Did the source configurations and transitions form a source-sensitive Trajectory?
2. Does that origin-typed Trajectory perform a bounded Frame-function in a later scene?

Only the first question belongs to this `COMPOSE` record.

## 2. Source and Claim Boundary

**Frame:** synthetic service-coordination history  
**Temporal scope:** P0–P5  
**Source granularity:** configurations, transitions, repeated subpath, occurrence-level burden redistribution, endpoint  
**Relative level:** source structures relative to one composed Trajectory  
**Claim Ceiling:** formation and historical load of the Trajectory only

The record may not decide a later Frame-function, Macro-Event function, Attractor function, organizational legitimacy, responsibility, intervention, or authority.

## 3. Typed Reconstruction

| Source object | Type | Temporal position | Constitutive contribution |
|---|---|---|---|
| `configuration.proj.baseline` | configuration | P0 | Ad hoc request allocation with concentrated coordination burden. |
| `transition.proj.review` | transition | P1 | A documented review introduces scheduled coordination. |
| `subpath.proj.rotation` | subpath | P2–P4 | Repeated rotation stabilizes a different continuation corridor. |
| `occurrence.proj.omega` | operator-typed occurrence | P3–P4 | Burden and exit costs are redistributed rather than erased. |
| `configuration.proj.endpoint` | configuration | P5 | Stable rotation with residual historical commitments. |

Constitutive relations preserve order, review-mediated change, repetition, burden redistribution, and endpoint residue.

## 4. COMPOSE Decision

**Selection Rule:** retain the baseline, review transition, repeated rotation, burden redistribution, and endpoint residue; exclude unrelated administrative detail.

**Ordering Rule:** `P0 < P1 < P2–P4 < P5`.

**Formation Rule:** form one Trajectory only where the review and repeated rotation change later burden, reversibility, and continuation options.

The target changes if the review is removed, the rotation never stabilizes, or burden redistribution is replaced by continued concentration. It is therefore source-sensitive.

## 5. Projection Boundary

A later governance meeting may use the completed Trajectory as a Frame for interpreting allocation. That proposition is neither contained in the Trajectory type nor generated by `COMPOSE`.

```text
COMPOSE(source history → Trajectory)
≠
PROJECT_AS(Trajectory → Frame-function in later context)
```

A later projection requires a separate record with:

- origin type preserved as `trajectory`;
- target context declared;
- Frame-function bounded;
- source-to-function trace;
- Counterfactual Sensitivity;
- separate Loss and alternatives;
- no authority inheritance.

This record therefore preserves a pending claim identifier but does not instantiate or validate a `PROJECT_AS` occurrence.
