# C52-NC1 — Competing Decompositions of a Compressed Handoff Occurrence

## 1. Case purpose

This Chapter-52 case instantiates a genuine, claim-relative `non_capture` result.

It tests whether one compressed occurrence can be opened into **one complete and decisive finer reconstruction** after multiple adequate bounded alternatives have been examined. It is not a demonstration that every decomposition fails, that the source object is mysterious, or that PMS-STRATA is globally incomplete.

The tested operation is one `DECOMPOSE` occurrence. The candidate models are rival decomposition routes inside the same fixed reference, Frame, temporal scope, and target granularity.

## 2. Source occurrence

The source object is the compressed occurrence `H0`, provisionally labelled “handoff failure,” over `H0-T0–H0-T4`.

The source packet states:

1. a partial material packet is sent at `H0-T0`;
2. the incoming role-holder lacks archive access until `H0-T2`;
3. ownership changes at `H0-T1`, but no accepted owner is recorded through `H0-T2`;
4. the handoff deadline passes at `H0-T3` without completed integration;
5. access and ownership are both stabilized before bounded recovery at `H0-T4`.

The source does **not** state:

- whether the partial packet was operationally sufficient;
- whether access or ownership changed first in an effective sense;
- whether either relation was decisive, merely contributory, or redundant;
- whether recovery depended jointly, sequentially, or independently on the two changes.

The coarse source function remains:

```text
coordination breakdown
→ deadline lapse
→ bounded recovery
```

## 3. Tested decomposition claim

The claim states:

```text
H0 admits one source-warranted finer reconstruction
that identifies the decisive constitutive internal relation
of the handoff failure.
```

The source object, occurrence type, Frame, temporal scope, and target granularity remain fixed throughout the test.

## 4. Adequate bounded attempts

### 4.1 Candidate A — access-blockage reconstruction

Candidate A reconstructs:

```text
partial transfer
→ unavailable archive access
→ incomplete integration
→ deadline lapse
```

This route has real PraxisPurchase. It exposes a material access topology hidden by the coarse label and remains source-traceable.

But it cannot establish that access blockage was the single constitutive relation. The ownership transition overlaps the same interval and remains independently load-bearing.

### 4.2 Candidate B — responsibility-transition reconstruction

Candidate B reconstructs:

```text
ownership change
→ no accepted owner
→ incomplete coordination
→ deadline lapse
```

This route also has real PraxisPurchase. It exposes a role and responsibility gap hidden by the coarse label and remains source-traceable.

But it cannot establish that the role gap was the single constitutive relation. Access remained unavailable during the same interval and remains independently load-bearing.

### 4.3 Candidate C — integrated reconstruction

Candidate C attempts:

```text
access blockage
↔ responsibility gap
→ one integrated constitutive mechanism
```

The present sources establish overlap, not direction, priority, mutual dependence, joint necessity, or causal hierarchy.

Forced integration would therefore invent the relation needed to make the candidate complete.

## 5. Counterfactual pressure

The candidates could be discriminated by changed-ground cases such as:

```text
access restored
+ ownership still unresolved
```

or:

```text
ownership accepted
+ access still unavailable
```

The present source packet supplies neither variation. Both access and ownership stabilize before recovery.

Therefore:

```text
candidate-local sensitivity: present
comparative sensitivity: untestable
```

## 6. Capture boundary

Captured:

- the same compressed occurrence `H0`;
- its coarse coordination-breakdown and recovery function;
- the partial-transfer, access-delay, role-transition, deadline-lapse, and recovery traces;
- a bounded access-topology reconstruction;
- a bounded responsibility-transition reconstruction;
- the overlap, source gaps, and re-entry conditions.

Uncaptured:

- one complete decisive internal reconstruction;
- comparative priority of access and responsibility relations;
- a source-supported constitutive or causal relation integrating both candidates;
- whether one candidate is constitutive and the other contributory or redundant.

The limiting condition is not mere missing information. It is the absence of the **specific discriminator required by the tested claim** after the relevant bounded alternatives and counterfactual pressures have been examined.

## 7. Why the result is Non-Capture

```text
Candidate A partially captures H0.
Candidate B partially captures H0.
Candidate C would require invented integration.
Coarse retention remains possible.
No adequate retained form of the same unique-decomposition claim remains.
```

Accordingly:

```text
selected Output Class:
non_capture

primary form:
granularity_non_capture
```

This is not `failed_transformation`: `DECOMPOSE` is the correct operation, the source reference remains stable, and both partial candidates are structurally valid.

This is not `partially_admissible`: the captured portions are rival partial claims that must remain separately bounded. They do not form an adequate retained portion of the same claim to one complete decisive decomposition.

This is not `claim_reduction_required`: reducing the claim to Candidate A, Candidate B, or coarse retention creates a different claim and requires its own record.

This is not `mandatory_stop`: no independent stop trigger decides the executed claim. Repetition on unchanged grounds is blocked by the claim disposition and re-entry contract.

## 8. Anti-shield contrast

A simple unsupported decomposition should map to `failed_transformation`, Claim Reduction, or Stop as appropriate. It must not be relabelled Non-Capture merely because detail is unavailable.

This case differs because:

1. the source object is stable;
2. the operation is correctly classified;
3. multiple relevant bounded routes were tested;
4. each major candidate preserves real source-supported structure;
5. counterfactual and calibration limits were identified;
6. the remaining capture limit belongs to the same delimited claim.

`C28-UNSUPPORTED-01`, `C28-FRAGMENT-01`, and `C40-N3` remain explicit anti-shield contrasts.

## 10. Re-entry

A later reconstruction requires materially changed grounds, for example:

- isolating variation between access and ownership;
- a timestamped accepted-owner record;
- evidence of transfer sufficiency;
- relation-specific recovery evidence;
- a narrower capture object.

```text
earlier NC0
+ changed grounds
+ new claim
→ new record NC1

NC1 ≠ NC0 erased
```

## 11. Authority boundary

Authority inheritance is prohibited.
