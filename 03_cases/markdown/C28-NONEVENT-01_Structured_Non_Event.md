It does not support motive, universal causality, person classification, legitimacy, application authority, or a target function.

**Decomposition question:** Which expected transition, bounded window, positive sub-events, blocked relations, and later effects make the review non-occurrence a structured Λ rather than missing information?

**Expected additional Praxis difference:** The finer reconstruction should preserve the Non-Event only if expectation, window, non-realization, and later relevance remain source-supported without inflating every absence into an event.

## 3. Components

- **review-expectation** — a declared expectation that review would occur.
- **review-window** — a bounded interval in which realization was due.
- **preparation-events** — agenda, scheduling, and evidence-preparation events.
- **blocking-relation** — a source-supported blockage preventing completion.
- **later-carryover** — a workaround that persists after the missed window.

These are components of the bounded occurrence or composite. They are not parts of the PMS operator type and do not become new primitives.

## 4. Finer Reconstruction

- `review-expectation → review-window` — **expectation-to-window relation**: the expectation is bounded by a declared completion window.
- `preparation-events → blocking-relation` — **preparation-to-blockage relation**: positive preparation occurs but does not complete the review.
- `blocking-relation → later-carryover` — **non-realization-to-carryover relation**: the blocked review leaves the workaround in place.

The positive result depends on these relations. Component presence alone would be insufficient.
